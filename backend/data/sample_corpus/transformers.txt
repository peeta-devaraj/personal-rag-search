The transformer architecture, introduced in the paper "Attention Is All You Need", replaced recurrent and convolutional layers with a self-attention mechanism that lets every token in a sequence attend directly to every other token. Self-attention computes a weighted combination of value vectors, where the weights come from a compatibility function between query and key vectors, allowing the model to capture long-range dependencies without the sequential bottleneck of recurrent networks.

Multi-head attention runs several attention computations in parallel, each with its own learned projections, so different heads can specialize in capturing different kinds of relationships between tokens, such as syntactic dependencies or coreference. The outputs of all heads are concatenated and linearly projected back to the model's hidden dimension. Transformers also rely on positional encodings to inject information about token order, since the self-attention operation itself is permutation-invariant.
